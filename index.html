Hi, I'm Johannes. I'm Head of Operations at Prometis Lab, where I lead software development. In my own time I write cycling software that combines noisy sensor data and physics into live numbers to help you read the road ahead.

I studied physics, then did a PhD in economics at Ghent University on how households react to income changes. Most of that PhD went into the pipeline that turns raw pseudonymised bank transactions into a research-ready dataset. That pipeline now runs at Prometis Lab every day. It's the kind of work I like most: a messy source of data, a model of what it measures, and software that others can build on.

Some things I've started:

  • Barberfish: native-feeling data field enhancements for the Hammerhead Karoo
  • Godot: a gradient-aware cycling ETA estimator
  • vanzelfsprekend: Matplotlib plots that speak for themselves, inspired by Tufte and Doumont
  • Ghent Festive 500: free social rides with Ghent's cycling clubs between Christmas and New Year; I founded it and organised the first four editions
